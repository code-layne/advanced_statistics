For each scenario, (a) recommend a design, (b) justify your choice, and

\textbf{1.} A psychologist tests whether mindfulness training reduces anxiety in college
students. She has 40 students; she knows men and women may respond differently.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=8pt]
  \item Recommended design: \makebox[7cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{7cm}}\hspace{46bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw1a, type=short, points=1, answer={randomized block design}, check=text, display={\bfseries block design (by gender)}]{2.2in}{0.2in}}}
  \item Justify:\par
        \gradedhide{\gradedhere{\gradedblank[id=hw1b, type=rubric, points=2, display={Gender may affect the response to mindfulness; blocking removes that variability, so the estimate is more precise.}]{\linewidth+6pt}{2in}}\writelines{9}}
  \item Draw a diagram:\gradedbelow{0.05in}{\kern-3pt\gradedblank[id=hw1c, type=rubric, points=3, display={40 students $\to$ Block 1 (20 men), Block 2 (20 women); within each block randomly assign 10 to mindfulness, 10 to control; compare anxiety.}]{\linewidth+6pt}{2in}}
  \vspace{2.1in}
\end{enumerate}

\tcbbreak
\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{2.} A dentist wants to compare two toothpaste brands for whitening. Each
patient has many teeth; they use Brand A on one side and Brand B on the other (randomly
assigned by coin flip).

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=8pt]
  \item Recommended design: \makebox[7cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{7cm}}\hspace{46bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw2a, type=short, points=1, answer={matched pairs design}, check=text]{2.2in}{0.2in}}}
  \item Why is this the most appropriate design?\par
        \gradedhide{\gradedhere{\gradedblank[id=hw2b, type=rubric, points=2, display={Each patient gets both brands, so diet, saliva, and brushing habits are controlled.}]{\linewidth+6pt}{2in}}\writelines{9}}
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{3.} A biologist tests whether a new growth hormone increases plant height. She
has 36 seedlings, all from the same variety and grown in the same conditions.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=8pt]
  \item Recommended design: \makebox[7cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{7cm}}\hspace{46bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw3a, type=short, points=1, answer={completely randomized design}, check=text, display={\bfseries CRD}]{2.2in}{0.2in}}}
  \item Why is blocking not needed here?\par
        \gradedhide{\gradedhere{\gradedblank[id=hw3b, type=rubric, points=2, display={Seedlings are homogeneous (same variety, same conditions): no nuisance variable to block on.}]{\linewidth+6pt}{2in}}\writelines{9}}
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{4.} Reflection: When is a CRD preferable to a block design, even if you know
a variable that affects the response?\\[4pt]
\gradedhide{\gradedhere{\gradedblank[id=hw4, type=rubric, points=2, display={When the variable can't be measured beforehand, is only weakly related to the response, or blocking's complexity outweighs the gain in precision.}]{\linewidth+6pt}{2in}}\writeline\\[1pt]\writelines{9}}
