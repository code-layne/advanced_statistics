\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{1.} 800 adults are randomly selected from a national voter registry and
surveyed about their political views and social media use. Heavier social media users
report more polarized political views.

\vspace{4pt}
Random assignment? \makebox[2cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{2cm}}\hspace{43bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw1a, type=short, points=1, answer=no, check=text]{0.33in}{0.17in}}} \quad Random selection? \makebox[2cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{2cm}}\hspace{46bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw1b, type=short, points=1, answer=yes, check=text]{0.6in}{0.17in}}}\\[4pt]
Causation? \makebox[2cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{2cm}}\hspace{43bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw1c, type=short, points=1, answer=no, check=text]{0.33in}{0.17in}}} \quad Generalize to? \makebox[7cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{7cm}}\hspace{46bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw1d, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries all national registered voters}]{2.5in}{0.17in}}}\\[4pt]
Conclusion: \gradedhide{\writeline\gradedright{\gradedhere{\gradedblank[id=hw1e, type=rubric, points=2, display={No random assignment: can't say social media causes polarization (confounding). Random sample: generalize to all national registered voters.}]{5.3in}{\gradedlinesheight{2}}}\hspace{\dimexpr 5.3in-3pt\relax}}\\[1pt]\writeline}

\textbf{2.} 60 student volunteers are randomly assigned to either an active-learning
classroom or a traditional lecture. Students in the active-learning group score
significantly higher on the final exam.

\vspace{4pt}
Random assignment? \makebox[2cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{2cm}}\hspace{43bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw2a, type=short, points=1, answer=yes, check=text]{0.33in}{0.17in}}} \quad Random selection? \makebox[2cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{2cm}}\hspace{46bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw2b, type=short, points=1, answer=no, check=text]{0.6in}{0.17in}}}\\[4pt]
Causation? \makebox[2cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{2cm}}\hspace{43bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw2c, type=short, points=1, answer=yes, check=text]{0.33in}{0.17in}}} \quad Generalize to? \makebox[7cm][l]{\vphantom{\blank{7cm}}\hspace{46bp}\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw2d, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries similar volunteers only}]{2.5in}{0.17in}}}\\[4pt]
Conclusion: \gradedhide{\writeline\gradedright{\gradedhere{\gradedblank[id=hw2e, type=rubric, points=2, display={Random assignment: active learning caused the higher scores.
