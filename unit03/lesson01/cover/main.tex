\boxguard[8]
\begin{remindbox}
\small
Before you believe a number, ask \textbf{how the data were collected}. If a random process chose
who is in the data, no group gets a head start. If \emph{people} chose --- a researcher
grabbing whoever is handy, or respondents volunteering --- some voices are louder than others
and the result leans one way. \textbf{A huge pile of responses does not fix that}: 2{,}400
volunteers are still volunteers. A small random sample beats a big biased one.
\end{remindbox}
