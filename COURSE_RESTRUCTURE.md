# Statistics — Restructured Course Outline

Shepherd · 2026–2027 · **8 units · 64 lessons** (8 per unit)

Every unit opens with **Lesson 0**, its "Introducing Statistics" hook lesson, followed
by seven content lessons (1–7). The old Units 8 and 9 combine into one unit; thin
setup/carry-out and CI/justify-claim lesson pairs merge. Each entry lists the current
lesson(s) it absorbs.

- **16 lessons merge into 8** (pairs): 1.3+1.4, 1.8+1.9, 2.7+2.8, 4.3+4.4, 4.5+4.6,
  4.8+4.9, 4.10+4.11, 6.5+6.6, 6.8+6.9, 6.10+6.11, 7.6+7.7, 7.8+7.9, 8.4+8.5,
  9.2+9.3, 9.4+9.5, 8.7+9.6 — each pair was a setup/carry-out or CI/justify split
  that teaches cleanly as one block.
- **Units 8 and 9 combine** into "Inference for Distributions and Relationships";
  9.1's hook folds into the new 8.0.
- **One new lesson**: 3.7 Skills Focus — Designing a Study (Unit 3 ran one short).
- **Renumbering**: every unit's lessons shift to 0–7; directory names, `main.tex`
  headers, unit covers, and cross-references ("from Lesson 5.1") all need updating.
- **Merged lessons need real authoring**, not concatenation: each merged pair's
  warmup, notes, activity, exit ticket, homework (+ keys) must be condensed to a
  single lesson's page budget under the one-page warmup/exit-ticket constraint.
