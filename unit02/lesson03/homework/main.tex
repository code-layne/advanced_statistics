\noindent A car dealer listed 18 used cars with 8 to 95 thousand miles. Price is in
thousands of dollars, mileage in thousands of miles:
\ $\widehat{\text{price}} = 32.0 - 0.24(\text{mileage})$. \ Answer every part \emph{in context}.

\vspace{0.02in}

\boxguard[14]
\begin{notesbox}{Part A --- Predicting with the Line}
\interlinepenalty=10000 % keep each prompt whole when the box breaks
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.9em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item Predict the price of a car with 50 thousand miles. Show your work.\gradedbelow[r]{5pt}{\textit{Price (\$1000s):}~\gradedblank[id=hw1, type=short, points=1, answer=20.0, check=numeric, tolerance=0.05]{0.9in}{0.2in}}
        \answerspace{0.7cm}{}
  \item A car with 50 thousand miles is listed at \$22{,}500. Find and interpret its residual.\gradedbelow[r]{5pt}{\gradedblank[id=hw2a, type=short, points=1, answer=2.5, check=numeric, tolerance=0.05]{1.0in}{0.9cm}}
        \answerspace{1.2cm}{}
        \answerspace{2.1in}{\normalcolor\mdseries\hspace*{12.5pt}\gradedblank[id=hw2b, type=rubric, points=2, display={Residual $= 22.5 - 20.0 = +2.5$: the car is listed \$2{,}500 above the line's prediction --- the line underpredicted its price.}]{\linewidth-12.5pt}{1.0cm}}
  \item Predict the price at 150 thousand miles. What went wrong, and why?\gradedbelow[r]{5pt}{\gradedblank[id=hw3a, type=short, points=1, answer=-4.0, check=numeric, tolerance=0.05]{1.0in}{0.9cm}}
        \answerspace{1.2cm}{}
        \answerspace{2.1in}{\normalcolor\mdseries\hspace*{12.5pt}\gradedblank[id=hw3b, type=rubric, points=2, display={$32.0 - 0.24(150) = -4.0$: a negative price. 150 thousand miles is far outside the 8--95 in the data --- extrapolation.}]{\linewidth-12.5pt}{1.0cm}}
\end{enumerate}
\end{notesbox}
