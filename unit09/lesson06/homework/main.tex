\textbf{Directions:} For each scenario, answer all three parts.
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item[(1)] Name the inference procedure.

\end{itemize}

\begin{notesbox}{Problem 1}

to estimate the mean SAT score for all seniors at this school with a 95\% confidence interval.

\vspace{0.08in}
(1) Procedure: \makebox[9cm][r]{\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw1a, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries\boldmath 1-sample $t$-interval for $\mu$}]{7cm}{0.2in}}}

\vspace{0.08in}
(2) Parameter being estimated: \gradedhide{\writeline\gradedright{\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw1b, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries\boldmath $\mu$ = true mean SAT score, all seniors here}]{3.3in}{0.2in}}}}

\vspace{0.08in}
(3) Conditions to check: \gradedhide{\writeline\\\gradedhere{\gradedblank[id=hw1c, type=rubric, points=2, display={\bfseries\boldmath Random; $n = 45 \ge 30$; $45 \le 10\%$ of seniors.}]{\linewidth+6pt}{\gradedlinesheight{1}}}\writelines{1}}

\vspace{0.08in}
(2) $H_0$: \gradedhide{\writeline\gradedright{\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw2b1, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries\boldmath All four $p = 0.25$}]{2.1in}{0.2in}}}}%

\vspace{0.08in}
(3) Conditions to check: \gradedhide{\writeline\\\gradedhere{\gradedblank[id=hw2c, type=rubric, points=2, display={\bfseries\boldmath Random; all expected $= 30 \ge 5$.}]{\linewidth+6pt}{\gradedlinesheight{1}}}\writelines{1}}

\begin{notesbox}{Problem 3}

national rate of 14\%.

\vspace{0.08in}
(1) Procedure: \makebox[9cm][r]{\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw3a, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries\boldmath 1-proportion $z$-test}]{7cm}{0.2in}}}

\vspace{0.08in}
(2) $H_0$: \gradedhide{\writeline\gradedright{\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw3b1, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries\boldmath $p = 0.14$}]{2.1in}{0.2in}}}}%
$H_a$: \gradedhide{\writeline\gradedright{\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw3b2, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries\boldmath $p \ne 0.14$}]{1.9in}{0.2in}}}}%

\vspace{0.08in}
(3) Conditions to check: \gradedhide{\writeline\\\gradedhere{\gradedblank[id=hw3c, type=rubric, points=2, display={\bfseries\boldmath Random; $80 \le 10\%$; $11.2, 68.8 \ge 10$.}]{\linewidth+6pt}{\gradedlinesheight{1}}}\writelines{1}}
\end{notesbox}

\vspace{0.08in}
(1) Procedure: \makebox[9cm][r]{\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw4a, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries\boldmath $t$-test for slope}]{7cm}{0.2in}}}

\vspace{0.08in}
(2) $H_0$: \gradedhide{\writeline\gradedright{\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw4b1, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries\boldmath $\beta = 0$}]{2.1in}{0.2in}}}}%
$H_a$: \gradedhide{\writeline\gradedright{\smash{\gradedblank[id=hw4b2, type=rubric, points=1, display={\bfseries\boldmath $\beta \ne 0$}]{1.9in}{0.2in}}}}%

\vspace{0.08in}
(3) Conditions to check: \gradedhide{\writeline\\\gradedhere{\gradedblank[id=hw4c, type=rubric, points=2, display={\bfseries\boldmath LINER: Linear, Independent, Normal residuals, Equal SD, Random.}]{\linewidth+6pt}{\gradedlinesheight{1}}}\writelines{1}}

\vspace{0.08in}
