\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.7em, itemsep=0.19in]

  \item \textbf{From last lesson.} One class's 40 quiz scores were drawn twice. With blocks of
        5 points the picture showed two separate peaks; with blocks of 20 points the very same
        40 scores showed one single hump.

        \vspace{5pt}
        Which of the two pictures is \emph{wrong}? \ans{Neither --- both are correct}

        \vspace{5pt}
        What should you check before you believe what any histogram looks like?
        \ans{the width of the blocks}

  \item Nine customers were timed at a coffee counter. Their waiting times, in minutes, are
        already written in order from shortest to longest.

        \vspace{4pt}
        \begin{center}
        1 \quad 2 \quad 2 \quad 3 \quad 4 \quad 4 \quad 5 \quad 7 \quad 12
        \end{center}
        \vspace{4pt}

        How many waits are listed? \ans{9} \qquad
        Counting in from both ends, which value lands exactly in the middle? \ans{4 min}

  \item Fifteen students were timed waiting for a ride home after school.

        \vspace{2pt}
        \begin{center}
        \begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.9]
          \draw[->, thick] (-0.2,0) -- (8.6,0);
          \foreach \x/\lab in {0/0, 1/2, 2/4, 3/6, 4/8, 5/10, 6/12, 7/14, 8/16} {
            \draw (\x,-0.07) -- (\x,0.07); \node[below, font=\scriptsize] at (\x,-0.1) {\lab};}
          \foreach \x/\n in {1/2, 1.5/3, 2/4, 2.5/2, 3/2, 3.5/1, 7.5/1} {
            \foreach \k in {1,...,\n} { \fill[navy] (\x,{0.22*\k}) circle (0.075); }}
          \node[font=\scriptsize] at (4.2,-0.6) {Minutes waited for a ride};
        \end{tikzpicture}
        \end{center}
        \vspace{2pt}

        Most of the waits sit between \ans{2} and \ans{7} minutes. \quad
        One wait sits far away from all the others: \ans{15} minutes.

        \vspace{6pt}
        In one sentence, describe where the other fourteen waits are compared with that one.\\[4pt]
        \ansline{The other fourteen are bunched down at the low end, between 2 and 7 minutes,}\\[1pt]\ansline{and then nothing at all happens until that single student out at 15 minutes.}

\end{enumerate}
